<p>Pleromastisk is an Android client for <a href="https://pleroma.social">Pleroma</a> instances, with a dark teal theme of its own. <b>The user interface is in Norwegian only.</b> It is made for social.gaute.space, but works with other Pleroma instances and, in principle, other servers with a Mastodon-compatible API.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>OAuth login – your password never passes through the app</li>
<li>Multiple accounts, also on different instances, with "Switch account" on the profile page</li>
<li>Timelines: Home, Local, Federated and your own lists</li>
<li>Reply, boost, favourite, bookmark, edit and delete your own posts</li>
<li>Compose posts with visibility, content warning, up to 4 images with alt text, Markdown and language tagging (nn, nb, en)</li>
<li>Custom emoji from the instance, with a searchable emoji picker</li>
<li>Image viewer with zoom and alt text, video thumbnails and link previews</li>
<li>Search for users and hashtags</li>
<li>Threads, profiles with profile fields, follow/unfollow and add to list</li>
<li>Notifications, including Pleroma emoji reactions, and polls</li>
<li>Swipe between tabs, compact bottom bar and double-tap to scroll to top</li>
<li>"About this instance" with the instance's own description and terms of service</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Not yet supported:</b> full-text search, chat, sending emoji reactions, push notifications, streaming, profile editing and in-app video playback.</p>

<p><b>Privacy:</b> No tracking or analytics. The app only talks to the instance you choose, and the access token is stored locally on the device.</p>
